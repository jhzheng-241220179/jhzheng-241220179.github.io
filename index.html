Computer Vision · 2026

Robust Multi-Image Stitching & Panorama Generation

Developed an automatic panorama generation pipeline supporting both pairwise and unordered multi-image stitching. The system combines feature extraction and matching, RANSAC-based homography estimation, image matching graphs and global geometric alignment to place multiple images onto a unified panorama canvas. Weighted blending is then applied to overlapping regions to produce visually coherent mosaics.
Python OpenCV NumPy NetworkX SIFT FLANN RANSAC Homography
